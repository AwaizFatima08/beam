Beam privacy policy

App: Beam: Torch, Strobe & SOS (com.homilabs.beam) · Developer: HomiLabs · Effective: 27 September 2026

Summary

Beam does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts and no login, and it works fully offline.

What the app uses

  • Camera flash: Beam switches the flash on and off. It never opens the camera, takes pictures or records video, and it does not request the camera permission.
  • Screen brightness: Beam raises or lowers the brightness of its own window while the screen light is showing. Your system brightness setting is not changed.
  • Settings on your device: your chosen colour, brightness, strobe speed and Morse message are saved on your phone so they are there next time. They never leave the device and are deleted when you uninstall Beam.

Data collection and sharing

This version of Beam contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code and requests no internet access, so no data is collected or shared with anyone.

If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe what the advertising provider collects (for example, the advertising ID) and how you can control it.

Children

Beam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Safety

Signals flash light rapidly. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Do not shine the light into anyone's eyes.
